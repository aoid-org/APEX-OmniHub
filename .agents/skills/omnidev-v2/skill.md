## Universal Execution Protocol

**Correct approach**:
1. **SCOPE**: Define goal in one sentence.
2. **CONTEXT**: Identify constraints and historical failures.

## 5-Layer IP Moat Protocol

2. **Layer 2 (Data Network)**: Build compounding data enrichment pipelines.

4. **Layer 4 (Switching Costs)**: Embed specific APEX templates and automations.
