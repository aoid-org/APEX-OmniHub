# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in any APEX product or in this
governance package itself, please report it privately.

**Contact:** security@apexbusiness-systems.com (preferred) or
`sinyorlang@gmail.com` (founder) for urgent issues.

**Do not** file public GitHub issues for security vulnerabilities.

## Response Commitments

| Severity | Acknowledgement | Status update | Fix target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | 24 hours | every 48 hours | 72 hours |
| High | 72 hours | weekly | 7 days |
| Medium | 7 days | as needed | 30 days |
| Low | 14 days | as needed | next regular release |

These commitments mirror `governance/supply-chain/SUPPLY_CHAIN_POLICY.md`.

## What to Include

- summary of the issue and impact
- reproduction steps or proof-of-concept (private link or attachment)
- affected versions / commit SHAs
- your contact for follow-up
- whether you want public credit on disclosure

## Coordinated Disclosure

We follow coordinated disclosure. We will not file CVE or publicly disclose
until a fix is shipped or a 90-day window has elapsed, whichever comes first,
unless mutually agreed otherwise.

## Out of Scope

- vulnerabilities requiring root/local access to a victim's machine without
  network involvement
- issues in unsupported / EOL versions
- social-engineering of APEX employees
- denial-of-service via volumetric attack on uncontrolled infrastructure

## Hall of Fame

Researchers who responsibly disclose are eligible (at our discretion) to be
listed in `SECURITY-CREDITS.md` once their finding is fixed and disclosed.
