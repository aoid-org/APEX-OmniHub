MAN Mode | Manual Approval Node
Manual
Approval
Node

MAN Mode is how high-risk operations get a human checkpoint — without freezing everything else. Every action is routed to the lane it has earned, and nothing crosses the RED line without your explicit approval.

Risk Lane Architecture

GREEN
Executes automatically. Trusted, low-risk actions flow without friction.
YELLOW
Runs with amplified tracking and tighter limits. We watch it closely.
RED
MAN Mode. Paused completely until a human explicitly approves. You stay in control.
BLOCKED
Hard stop. Security event raised. No side effects. No exceptions.
