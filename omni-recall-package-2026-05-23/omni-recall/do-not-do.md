# Do Not Do
**Version:** 1.1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-26 | **Author:** Lead AI Co-Founder | **Status:** Audited & Active

- **No Inaccessible History Claims:** Do not pretend the full historical ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini account has been ingested unless exports or connectors actually provided it.
- **No Weight or Hook Claims:** Do not claim this modifies GPT base weights, hidden system internals, or platform-native hooks.
- **No Temporary Preference Elevation:** Do not store one-off emotional tone choices as permanent preferences without repeat evidence.
- **No Mixed Audit Verification:** Do not mix uploaded audit claims with independently verified operational truth.
- **No Isolated Corrections:** Do not let correction notes stay isolated; propagate broad corrections to canonical pages or directives.
- **No Duplicated Concepts:** Do not create parallel pages for the same concept unless separation is intentional and named.
- **No Busywork / Noise:** Do not produce busywork dashboards, vanity metrics, or constant chatter.
- **No Governance Bloat:** Do not expand governance docs into bloated context-hogging essays.
